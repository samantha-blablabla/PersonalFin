## VSA Rules Implemented

### Shark Detection Logic
- 🚀 **Big Money In**: Price > +2% AND Volume > 1.5x AvgVolume
- ⚠️ **Distribution**: Price < 0% AND Volume surge
- 💎 **Low Supply**: Price đi ngang (±0.5%) AND Volume < 0.7x AvgVolume
- 🚨 **Pump & Dump**: Price tăng mạnh KHÔNG kèm volume
